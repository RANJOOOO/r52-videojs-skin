How to read it

Red ink is what has played. The faint pencil line ahead of the pen is the rest of the sound, drawn from the audio before you press Run, so loud and quiet parts are visible before you get there.

Gauges

BUF is seconds buffered ahead of the pen, DROP is dropped video frames from the browser, LVL is the sound level under the pen. The lamp is green when running, amber while loading, red on a fault.

Keys

Space or K run and hold. Left and right move 5 seconds, up and down set volume, M mutes, F is fullscreen, 0 to 9 jump through the chart.
